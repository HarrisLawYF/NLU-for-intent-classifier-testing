## Contact Broker (Loose)

**Definition:**

This intent captures a wide range of user expressions related to **getting in touch with or inquiring about real estate professionals**. The user's language might be indirect, informal, or conversational.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- **Vague Inquiries**: Indirect questions about speaking to a person, getting help, or asking for a human.
- **Informal Requests**: Casual ways of asking for contact, like "hit me up" or "send me their details."
- **Implied Need**: Statements that imply a need for a broker, such as asking about the next steps after finding a property.
- **General Queries**: Questions about who to talk to for more information.

**Key Notes:**

- This version is designed to be more inclusive and catch queries that are less direct than the standard `contact_broker` intent.
- Assume that if a user is asking for a person or help regarding a property, they likely want to speak to a broker.

**Examples:**

- "Can someone help me with this house?"
- "Who do I talk to about buying a place?"
- "I'm interested, what's the next step?"
- "Just get me a person."
- "Send me the agent's info."
