## Contact Broker

**Definition:**

This intent covers all user inquiries and actions related to **contacting a real estate broker or agent**. This includes, but is not limited to:

- **Direct Contact**: Requests to speak with a broker, be connected with an agent, or get contact information (e.g., phone number, email).
- **Scheduling Appointments**: Inquiries about scheduling a meeting, viewing, or consultation with a broker.
- **Broker Information**: Questions about finding a specific broker or asking for details about available brokers.
- **Callback Requests**: Asking for a broker to call them back.

**Key Notes:**

- Any request that implies a desire to communicate directly with a broker or agent should be classified under this intent.
- This is distinct from general property searches, which should be classified under `search_property`.

**Examples:**

- "I want to talk to a broker about this property."
- "Can you connect me with an agent?"
- "Please have a broker call me back."
- "How can I schedule a viewing with an agent?"
