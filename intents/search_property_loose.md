## Search Property (Loose)

**Definition:**

This intent captures a wide range of user inquiries related to the specifics, features, or characteristics of a product, service, or item. The user's language might be informal, indirect, or less precise than a direct technical question. It covers curiosity about what a product can do, what it's made of, or how it compares to expectations.

- **General Features & Capabilities**: Vague questions about what a product can do, its battery performance, speed, or general quality.
- **Physical Attributes**: Informal questions about a product's material, look and feel, or whether it has certain physical parts.
- **Implied Comparisons & Concerns**: Questions that imply a concern or a comparison, like asking if a phone's battery is "good" or if a piece of clothing will "last long".
- **Everyday Compatibility**: Practical questions about how a product fits into their life, e.g., "can I plug my old headphones into this?", "will this case get dirty easily?".

**Key Notes:**

- This intent is designed to be broad and catch queries that are conceptually about product properties, even if they are not phrased as a clear question for a specific attribute.
- It can include questions that are on the border of being about customer support or general FAQs but are still rooted in a product's specific characteristics.

**Examples:**

- "How long does the iPhone battery last?"
- "Is the new Samsung phone good for taking pictures?"
- "Can I use the iPhone all day without it dying?"
- "Can I plug my headphones into the new Galaxy?"
- "What's this shirt made of? Will it shrink?"
- "Is this laptop fast enough for gaming?"
- "Will this phone case get yellow over time?"
- "Is the screen on the new Dell laptop bright?"
