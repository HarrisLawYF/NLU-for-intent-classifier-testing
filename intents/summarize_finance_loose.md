## Summarize Finance (Loose)

**Definition:**

This intent broadly covers user requests for simplified overviews or general summaries of financial matters. The language used might be informal, conversational, or less precise, reflecting a desire for quick insights rather than detailed reports. It includes inquiries about personal money, company performance, market trends, or investment health, often phrased casually.

- **Casual Financial Check-ins**: Questions about recent spending, how much money is left, or general financial status.
- **Quick Company Updates**: Requests for the "gist" of a company's financial situation or recent news.
- **Market Vibes**: Asking for a general sense of how the stock market is doing or what's happening in the economy.
- **Investment Snapshot**: Informal questions about how investments are performing without needing specific numbers.
- **Account Overview**: Simple requests to see what's going on with bank accounts or credit cards.

**Key Notes:**

- This intent is designed to catch a wide variety of less formal requests for financial summaries.
- It prioritizes ease of understanding and quick answers over comprehensive data.

**Examples:**

- "What did I spend money on recently?"
- "How much did I blow last month?"
- "Can you give me the lowdown on my finances?"
- "What's the latest with Apple's money situation?"
- "How's the market looking today?"
- "Did my stocks do well this quarter?"
- "Just give me a quick look at my bank account."
- "What's the financial buzz right now?"
- "Is Google making money?"
- "What's on my credit card statement?"
