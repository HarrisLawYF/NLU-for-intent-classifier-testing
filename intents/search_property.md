## Search Property

**Definition:**

This intent is triggered when a user is looking for specific information, attributes, or properties of a product or item. The query often contains the product name and the specific property they are interested in.

- **Product Specifications**: Inquiries about technical specs like screen size, resolution, processor type, RAM, storage, etc.
- **Product Features**: Questions about whether a product has a certain feature, such as being waterproof, having a specific port, or supporting a particular technology.
- **Material and Composition**: Questions about what a product is made of.
- **Compatibility**: Inquiries about whether a product is compatible with another product or standard.

**Key Notes:**

- Queries that are direct questions about a specific, factual attribute of a product should be classified under this intent.
- Broader questions about product recommendations or comparisons between products might fall under a different intent.

**Examples:**

- "What is the battery life of the new iPhone?"
- "Does the Samsung Galaxy S24 have a headphone jack?"
- "Tell me the screen resolution of the Dell XPS 15."
- "How much RAM does the MacBook Pro have?"
- "Is this shirt made of cotton?"
- "Is this phone case compatible with wireless charging?"
