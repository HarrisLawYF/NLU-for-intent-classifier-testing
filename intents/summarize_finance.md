## Summarize Finance

**Definition:**

This intent is triggered when a user requests a summary or overview of financial information. This can include personal finances, company financial reports, market summaries, investment performance, or any other data related to financial transactions and status.

- **Personal Finance Summaries**: Requests for summaries of personal spending, income, budget adherence, or account balances.
- **Company Financials**: Summaries of earnings reports, balance sheets, income statements, or financial news for specific companies.
- **Market Overviews**: Requests for summaries of stock market performance, sector trends, or economic indicators.
- **Investment Performance**: Summaries of portfolio performance, individual stock/fund returns, or investment news.
- **Transaction Summaries**: Overviews of recent transactions, categorized spending, or recurring payments.

**Key Notes:**

- The intent focuses on providing a concise overview rather than detailed analysis or specific data points.
- Queries asking for raw data or in-depth calculations should be classified under a different, more analytical intent.

**Examples:**

- "Summarize my spending for last month."
- "Give me a brief of the Q3 earnings report for Apple."
- "What's the market summary for today?"
- "How did my investment portfolio perform this quarter?"
- "Can you give me a quick overview of my current account balance and recent transactions?"
- "Summarize the latest financial news."
- "What's the financial health of Google?"
- "Show me a summary of my credit card statement."
